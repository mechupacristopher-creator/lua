# Eze Hub — RIVALS

Combat, cosmetics and clip hub for [RIVALS](https://www.roblox.com/games/17625359962/).

## Loader

```lua
loadstring(game:HttpGet("https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mechupacristopher-creator/lua/main/RIVALS/rivals_hub.lua"))()
```

Toggle the panel with **RightControl**.

## Features

**Combat**
- Silent Aim: shots go to the closest visible enemy in the FOV circle
- Aimbot: hard lock or smoothed, aim key, head or torso, FOV circle, wall check
- Triggerbot with delay
- No Recoil and No Spread

**Visuals**
- ESP: highlight, name, health and distance, team check

**Skins**
- Unlock All Cosmetics: every Skin, Wrap, Charm, Finisher and Emote in the game's own Equipment menu
- Equipped picks are remembered across matches and rejoins (client side only)

**Player**
- Speed boost, jump boost, bunny hop

**Clipfarming**
- Spoof your level, win streak, rank and name on nametags, the 1v1 scoreboard and results (client side only)

**Misc**
- Auto Rematch and Auto Play Again
- Claim free rewards
- Unload Hub

## Notes

- Everything starts **off**.
- Running the script again replaces the previous copy.
- The hub reloads itself after teleporting into a match and keeps your settings in `rivals_hub_cfg.json`.
